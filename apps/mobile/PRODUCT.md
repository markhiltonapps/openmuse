# Product

## Platform

web

## Users

A small first group (a "few people"), mixed: some want help with home and family life, some also
run a business or side job. They use the installed web app (PWA) on a phone or a computer, often
by voice. Each person has their own agent, "Neddy" by default, which they can rename (the owner's
is "AI Todd").

## Product Purpose

Neato_Muse is a personal AI agent that manages a person's personal and business life in the
background: email, calendar, reminders, bills and statements, research, shopping, health, family
plans, social posts and connected apps. Success is the person doing less: less typing, fewer
decisions, fewer things remembered by them instead of by Neddy.

## Positioning

The agent pursues the person, not the other way round. It gets to know them by talking, connects
their apps with them step by step, and then acts ahead of them, asking only when something pays,
sends or changes on their behalf.

## Operating Context

- Mostly spoken: hands-free voice mode, spoken replies, a mic on answers. Typing is the fallback.
- Neddy works while the app is closed: routines, watches, email rules, app alerts, commitments,
  reminders, meal check-ins, Ideas and background jobs that use websites.
- Approvals: steps that pay, send, submit, book or change something wait for the person's OK
  ("Needs you"); chosen low-risk actions can be "Always allowed".
- Apps connect through Composio (Outlook, Gmail, Google Calendar, Slack and many more).

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Autonomy (confirmed): Neddy does low-risk things on his own and tells the person afterwards;
  he asks before anything that pays, sends or changes something.
- First interview (confirmed): about five spoken minutes at first sign-in, then one or two
  questions at natural moments over the following days. Answers can stay unfilled; the interview
  can always continue.
- Voice (confirmed direction): a natural-sounding paid voice for Neddy's speech, with turn-taking;
  the person's speech is still recognised by the browser. Voice works in the web app only today.
- Reaching people when the app is closed (confirmed): push notifications, text messages and email.
  Phone calls are out of scope for now.
- Budget: the owner wants low per-person costs (roughly $20 a month at the low end); no per-person
  cost caps are enforced yet.
- Undecided: pricing; which text-message and voice providers; native (non-web) apps.

## Brand Commitments

- Product name Neato_Muse; the agent is Neddy by default, with a friendly robot mascot and an
  animated avatar. The help page calls Neddy "he".
- Marketing pages follow the Neato Ventures brand guidelines. The app keeps its own existing look.

## Evidence on Hand

No customers, testimonials or usage numbers yet. Do not invent any.

## Product Principles

1. Neddy reaches out first; the person shouldn't have to remember to open the app.
2. Talk, don't type: every question can be answered by voice, and one word ("yes", "later") is
   enough wherever possible.
3. Show what he knows: everything Neddy learned about the person is visible, editable and
   forgettable.
4. Ask only what matters: never pay, send or change something without an OK, and don't ask about
   what can safely be done and reported.
5. Always resumable: nothing in setup blocks using the app, and skipped questions come back later.
