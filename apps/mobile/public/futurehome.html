★ 01 · Today ★

Already on the job.

Beta

In beta and invite-only. Ask in plain words, typed or out loud. Anything that sends, buys, books or changes something waits for your tap.

  • Chat with Neddy

    Type or talk, the way you would to a helpful assistant. Tell him about yourself and he remembers what matters. Pick his tone (warm, concise or thoughtful), rename him, or give him a new look.

    Try ›“What’s on tomorrow?”

  • Email and calendar

    Reads, searches and sums up your Gmail or Outlook, and drafts replies for you to check. He can tell you when new mail lands, or follow a rule like “When Dana emails, summarize it.” He lines up calendar changes for your OK. Forward mail to his own address to hand over the work, attachments included.

    Try ›“Summarize my unread email and draft replies to anything urgent.”

  • Web errands that ask first

    He searches and reads the web, and works in a browser of his own. He signs in with passwords you save in the app, never in chat, and never sees them. He watches pages for price drops, finds open tables and gets orders ready. Buying is off until you turn it on, with a cap for each purchase and each month. Tap Take control to finish anything yourself.

    Try ›“Watch this page and tell me when the price drops.”

  • Spaces for health, family and social media

    • Health

      Log a meal from a photo or a sentence, answer meal check-ins, see each day and week on a chart, and follow a guided workout.

    • Family

      The week on one board, with dinners and recipes, groceries, the schedule and chore stars.

    • Social media

      Neddy plans and schedules your posts, and each one waits for your OK.

  • Memory you control

    He suggests things to remember and asks before he keeps them. See, fix or forget any memory, any time. Import your past chats from ChatGPT or Claude and he can search them.

  • Voice

    Tap the sound-wave button and talk it through, hands-free. Hear the answers out loud, and tap Interrupt to cut in. Choose your microphone right next to the mic.

  • Files and reports

    He reads PDFs, Word, Excel, PowerPoint and photos. He writes PDFs, Word documents, spreadsheets and slide decks, and makes small mini-apps you can share with a link. Every report, comparison and plan he saves waits in Files. He can bring in files from Google Drive, OneDrive and Dropbox too.

  • Take-me-there buttons

    Ask where something is, like “Where’s my food log?”, and a button under his answer takes you straight there. Back to chat brings you back.

★ 02 · What’s next ★

Hand off the paperwork.

This isn’t built yet. Here’s what it will do. You’ll get a private browser of your own that stays signed in to the sites you use, and Neddy gets the tools to work on documents and spreadsheets. Then he can take whole jobs off your list and keep at them while you’re away.

Coming to Neato_MuseNeato Muse

Legwork

Coming soonNot built yet

What you’ll hand off

  • Statements

    Each month, fetch your bank and card statements and file them in Files.

  • Bills

    Sign in to a biller’s site, find the bill and get everything ready up to the Pay button. You tap Pay.

  • Research

    Longer research that runs for hours and saves what it finds as a report.

  • Documents and spreadsheets

    Fill in a PDF form, update a spreadsheet, or tidy a Word document and turn it into a PDF, with no desktop computer needed.

  • Watch and act

    Keep an eye on pages you have to sign in to see, like an order’s status or a renewal date, and act when something changes, within your rules.

  • Repeat jobs

    Do a job once with you. Next time, he follows the same steps on his own.

How it will work

  1. 1

    Step 1: Sign in once

    You sign in to a site yourself, in your private browser. It stays signed in, kept apart from everyone else’s. Neddy never sees your passwords.

  2. 2

    Step 2: Hand it over

    Ask once, or set it to repeat. He reads each page’s words and buttons, fills in what’s needed and saves the results in Files.

  3. 3

    Step 3: Approve what matters

    Paying, submitting, sending and deleting wait for you in Needs you, with the bill or form attached.

  4. 4

    Step 4: Next time, a recipe

    The first run records the steps. Later runs follow them, and he only stops to think if the site has changed.

An illustration of a feature that isn’t built yet. The dates and times are examples.

Where it will stop and ask

Some sites turn automated browsers away. Some banks send a code to your phone, or ask you to approve each sign-in in their app. When that happens, Neddy stops and asks you, or you tap Take control and finish it yourself.

It will arrive in the same app. Request an invite to start with what’s there today.

★ 03 · Staying safe ★

You still keep the keys.

The new work follows the rules you already have, plus new ones for money. Each rule says whether it’s here today or coming soon.

Sign-ins he never seesBeta
Save a site’s password in the app, never in chat. Neddy never sees it. It’s typed in only on that site.
Approve first, in Needs youBeta
Sending, buying, booking and changing things already wait for your tap. Paying, submitting and deleting will too, with the bill or form attached.
Take controlBeta
Take over his browser any time to sign in or finish something yourself.
Known-biller rulesComing soon
Neddy will pay on his own only when every rule holds: the biller is on a list only you can add to, the amount is under your limit and close to your recent bills, it’s paid once from the account you chose, and he found the bill on the biller’s own site, not through an email link. Anything else waits for you. He never types card numbers.
Official connections firstComing soon
For statements and card payments, he’ll use your bank’s or biller’s official connection where there is one, and the browser only when there isn’t.
A page can’t move your moneyComing soon
A web page can hide instructions, like “pay this person”. That’s why money only goes to billers on your list, and everything else waits for you.
Everything on the recordComing soon
Every payment and submission is logged, with a notice to you and a saved receipt.

★ 04 · Light by design ★

Built light, on purpose.

Coming soon

The costly way to give an AI agent hands is a whole computer for every person, with the AI studying pictures of the screen. We’re building it the light way, so it can stay affordable.

  • Shared machine
    Yours

    Shared machines, private browsers

    Many people’s browsers share one machine, each sealed off with its own sign-ins. Nobody pays for a whole computer that sits idle.

  • Pages read as text

    Neddy reads a page’s words and buttons instead of looking at pictures of it. That takes fewer, smaller steps.

  • Recipes for repeat jobs

    The first run records the steps. Later runs follow them without calling on the AI, unless the site has changed.

  • Watches that wait quietly

    A watch checks the page by itself and only calls on Neddy when something changes.

  • A full desktop only when needed

    For the rare job that needs a desktop program, he uses a full computer for that job alone.

Pricing hasn’t been announced yet.
