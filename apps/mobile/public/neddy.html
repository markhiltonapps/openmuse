Private to you.

Neddy is your agent, under your own account. What he knows about you is yours to see, change and delete, and he asks before anything goes out, unless you’ve said that kind of thing is always fine.

  • By invite only.Tap Request access, and the person who runs it lets you in.
  • He asks before he acts.Emails, bookings and purchases show up for your approval first, unless you’ve said a kind of thing is always fine. Purchases always ask.
  • Money and sign-in codes need a tap.Your voice alone is never enough for those.
  • Card and ID numbers are hidden from the AI.Card numbers, Social Security numbers, bank account and routing numbers, passport and driver’s licence numbers, passwords, PINs and secret keys are found by their shape and hidden before the AI model sees them. That covers what you type, your email, files, web pages and connected apps. He tells you in one short line when he hid something.
  • Where that stops.It lowers the risk. It isn’t a guarantee. Your own message stays in your chat history; only the model never sees the number. On a live call the voice service hears what you say, so speech isn’t covered. Numbers spelled out in words, and ID formats from other countries with no label beside them, can slip through.
  • What he remembers is in plain sight.About you, people, trackers and meetings, all editable. Under each answer in chat, “What I used” shows where he looked, whether he hid something private (like a card number or password) before reading it, and what he knows about you, with a Forget button on each item. He keeps your About you page in mind for every answer, so the list can’t say which facts one answer leaned on. Download everything or reset at any time.
  • Apps connect one at a time, with your sign-in.Gmail, Outlook, your calendar. Each one you choose, and each one you can disconnect.
