How to use Neato_Muse

Hi, I'm Neddy, your personal AI agent. I read and draft your email, keep your calendar, research things, keep an eye on the web, set reminders and run errands. I always check with you before I send, buy or change anything.

Get started in 4 steps

It takes about five minutes.

Sign in

Tap the link in your invite email. There's no password. Links work once, so ask for a new one on the sign-in screen when you need it.

Add it to your home screen

  • iPhone: in Safari, tap Share, then Add to Home Screen.
  • Android: in Chrome, tap ⋮, then Install app.

Connect your apps

Open Apps, search for Gmail, Outlook, Google Calendar and more, and tap Connect. You sign in to each app yourself.

Say hi

Type in Chat or tap the sound-wave button to talk. Tell Neddy about yourself and he'll remember what matters.

Things you can ask

Talk to Neddy like you would a helpful assistant. A few ideas:

Email

You have 3 that need you. I drafted 2 replies for you to check.
Calendar
What's on tomorrow? Move my 3pm to Thursday.
Two meetings and a dentist visit. Here's the change, tap Approve to send it.
Restaurants
Find a table for 4 at Nobu on Friday at 7:30.
7:15 and 8:00 are open. Here's the OpenTable link with it all filled in.
Reminders
Remind me to call Mom on Sunday at 5.
Done. You'll see it on your Feed with your other reminders.

Every weekday at 8am, send me a rundown of my day.
Routine set. You'll get the first one tomorrow morning.
Watch the web
Watch this page and tell me when the price drops.
I'm checking it regularly and will ping you when it changes.
Documents

Here it is. It's saved in Files & media too.

That's a pothos. Water it when the top inch of soil is dry.

Something not working?

Tap a question to see the answer.

My sign-in link doesn't work
Each link works once. Invite links last 3 days and sign-in links 15 minutes. Ask for a new one on the sign-in screen. Using the app from your iPhone home screen? Links open in Safari, so press and hold the link in the email, tap Copy, and paste it on the sign-in screen.
Neddy says an app needs reconnecting
Apps sometimes sign you out. Go to Apps › Apps, find the app and tap Connect to sign in again.
A website turned Neddy away
Some sites, like OpenTable, block automated browsers. Neddy gives you a link with the date, time and party size already filled in. Tap it to finish on your phone.
I'm not getting notifications
On iPhone, add Neato_Muse to your home screen, open it from there, then turn on notifications in Apps › Alerts. Check that your phone's settings allow notifications for it too.
Neddy got something wrong
Tell him in chat, like "No, I prefer morning meetings." To fix something he remembers, go to Apps › Agent › Memory.
It says the worker is offline
Background work pauses for a moment, often during an update. Your saved work carries on by itself when it reconnects, so try again in a minute or two.
The app icon or look didn't update
Close and reopen the app. For a new home-screen icon, remove the app from your home screen and add it again.
How do I invite a friend?
The person who runs your Neato_Muse invites people in Apps › Account › People. Each person gets their own private space and their own agent.
Still stuck?
Ask the person who invited you to Neato_Muse. It helps to say what you tapped and what you saw.
