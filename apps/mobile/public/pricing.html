What counts, and what happens at the limit.

Everything is counted per month. A minute is a minute on a live call, silence included. A message is one thing you send, however long his answer. A call uses your minutes of calls, not messages. A meeting hour is recording time. A job is one errand you hand him, however many sites he visits.

When you reach a limit, Neddy tells you when it comes back. If you run out of minutes of calls, you can keep typing. For more room, email hello@neatoventures.com and a person will help. Paid plans and extras can’t be bought yet.

Questions

Is there a free plan?

Yes. New people start on Free: 5 minutes of calls, 100 messages, 30 minutes of meetings and 1 background job a month. Neddy is invite-only and billing isn’t switched on yet, so you can’t pay today. Request access to ask for an invite.

Does anything roll over?

No. Unused minutes, messages, meeting hours and jobs don’t carry into the next month. If you run short, email hello@neatoventures.com and a person will help. Buying extras or a bigger plan starts when billing does.

Can I change or cancel?

Billing isn’t on yet, so there’s nothing to cancel today. Once it is, email hello@neatoventures.com and we’ll change or stop your plan. You can download your data first.

Do you train on my chats?

No. Neddy doesn’t train AI models on your chats, calls, meetings or files. To answer you, your words do go to the AI and voice services we use, under their business terms. Before that, Neddy hides card, bank and ID numbers, passwords and PINs he can recognise in what you type, your email, files and web pages. It lowers the risk; it isn’t a guarantee, and it can’t cover your voice on a live call.
