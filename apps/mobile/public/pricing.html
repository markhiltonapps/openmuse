What counts, and what happens at the edge.

A minute is a minute on a live call, silence included. A message is one thing you send, however long his answer. A meeting hour is recording time. A job is one errand you hand him, however many sites he visits.

When you reach a cap, Neddy says so, out loud if you’re on a call. Run out of call minutes and you can keep typing. Ask us for the bigger plan or the extras below. What you’ve stored stays yours to read.

Do unused minutes roll over?

No. Each month starts fresh. Start with the smaller one and move up when he says you’ve reached the edge.

Can I change or cancel?

Yes. Say it to Neddy, or email hello@neatoventures.com, and we’ll change or stop it. What you’ve stored stays until you download or delete it.

Is there a free trial?

No trial. There’s nothing to pay until you’re in and choose a plan, and early access is by invite.

Next to Meta Muse.

Muse sells tokens, 500 million a week on Power and 3 billion on Maximum. A token is a fragment of a word, and everything the agent reads and writes counts. A message like ours is about 70,000 tokens, so 500 million a week is roughly 7,000 messages, and for typed chat Muse is effectively unlimited. Neddy’s $20 plan gives you 150 messages a month. We won’t pretend that’s more.

What the same $20 buys with Neddy that Muse’s pages don’t list: an hour of live calls, three hours of recorded meetings with notes, five errands he runs for you in the background, memory you can read and change, and a person at hello@neatoventures.com who answers when you write.
