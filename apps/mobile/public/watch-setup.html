Set up your watch

Your phone talks to the watch over Bluetooth, so your phone stays on its own Wi-Fi.

This browser can't use Bluetooth.

On an Android phone, open this page in Chrome. On an iPhone, use the watch's other setup instead: tap the watch's screen and scan the new code.

  1. Keep the watch on its QR screen and turn Bluetooth on.
  2. Tap the button, then pick your watch from the list (it is called Neddy).

Pick your home Wi-Fi. The watch works on 2.4 GHz Wi-Fi.
