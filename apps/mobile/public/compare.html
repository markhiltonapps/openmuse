Where they’re ahead.

We’d rather you knew. The big four have more people, more models and more countries. Here’s what that buys them, and what we do about it.

  • Their own models.Neddy runs on the best models we can rent, and can switch when a better one appears. You’re never stuck with one lab.
  • Native phone apps.Neddy runs in the browser and can be added to your home screen. There’s no store app yet.
  • Open sign-up.Neddy is by invite while it’s small, so every person gets a real human behind the door.
  • Built-in integrations.Neddy connects Gmail, Outlook, calendars, Drive, Dropbox, Spotify, Brex and more through a growing app directory, and can work on websites in his own browser.
