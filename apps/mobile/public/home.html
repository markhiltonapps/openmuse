★ 01 · What it does ★

Inbox read. Draft written. Table for two found.

Ask in plain words. It does the reading, the searching and the first draft.

  • Email

    Reads, searches and summarizes your Gmail or Outlook, and drafts replies for you to check. It can ping you the moment new mail arrives.

    Try ›“Find the invoice from the printer and summarize it.”

  • Calendar

    Reads your calendar, then lines up new events and changes for you to approve.

    Try ›“Add lunch with Sam on Thursday at noon.”

  • The web

    Searches and reads the web, works in its own browser, and watches pages for price drops and updates. You can take over its browser whenever you need to.

    Try ›“Tell me when this jacket drops in price.”

  • Routines

    Set it once: “Every weekday at 8 am, send me a rundown of my day.” One-off reminders too.

  • Files

    Reads PDFs, Word, Excel and photos. Writes finished PDF and Word documents. Imports from Google Drive, OneDrive and Dropbox.

  • Morning feed

    News with pictures on the topics you choose. It learns from your reactions.

  • Ideas and goals

    Each day it suggests something it could do for you, based on your goals and recent work. It tracks those goals and the routines that serve them.

  • Restaurants

    Finds open tables on OpenTable with the date, time and party size filled in. You pick the time and book it.

  • Shopping

    Snap a photo of a product and it finds where to buy one. Buying stays off until you switch it on.

  • Health

    Log a meal from a photo or a sentence. Follow a guided workout.

  • Subscriptions

    Finds recurring charges in your email receipts and drafts the cancellation emails.

★ 02 · See it work ★

From ask to approve.

Illustrative exchanges. The names and times are examples.

  1. Step 1: You ask

    Type it or say it. Ask for something now, or set it once as a rule or a routine.

  2. Step 2: It gets to work

    It reads, searches and drafts in the background, then brings you what’s ready.

  3. Step 3: You tap approve

    Check the draft, change it or turn it down. It goes ahead only when you say so.

★ 03 · Approvals & privacy ★

You keep the keys.

It does the legwork. The decisions, the limits and the data stay with you.

Approve first
Sends, bookings and calendar changes wait for your Approve. Low-risk actions you trust can be set to Always allow. Purchases always ask.
Spending limits
Buying through connected apps is off until you turn it on. Then each purchase has a cap, and so does each month.
Private workspace
Your chats, memory, files and connected apps are kept in a workspace of your own.
Memory you control
It suggests things to remember and asks before it keeps them. See, edit or forget any memory, any time.
Your sign-ins
You sign in to each app yourself. If the agent’s browser reaches a sign-in page, tap Take control and finish it by hand.
Your data
Export your data, delete a single message, or reset everything.
