Pricing questions (owner)
- Per-minute bottom line: ~1.6¢ a minute on our own voice (plan on 2¢); a quiet minute 0.2¢;
  a chatty one ~1.9¢; meetings 10¢ an hour; OpenAI's voice 5¢ a minute.
- Tokens vs Meta Muse's plans (Power $20 / 500M tokens a week, Maximum $100 / 3B): measured
  costs are ~$0.70 per million tokens on Sonnet chat, ~$0.35 on Haiku, ~$0.02 on DeepSeek flash.
  At a 60% margin the $20 plan buys 2.5M tokens a week on Sonnet, 5M on Haiku, 90M on DeepSeek.
  Muse's figure is a ceiling nobody reaches (a full-size message every second, all day); a
  heavy chatter uses ~15M a week. Advised pricing on what people do, with tokens as fine print.
