KNOWN ISSUES
------------
- The calendar groups posts by the device's time zone; the digest uses the agent's. Near
  midnight while travelling, a post can show on the neighbouring day (same as the family board).
- save_week_results can't check which apps are connected; the "only real numbers" rule is in
  the agent's instructions, not enforced in code.
- Posts queued in a scheduler app (Postiz, Buffer, Hootsuite) don't appear on the calendar; the
  empty calendar says so and offers to ask the agent what's scheduled.
- Plans written before this change have no start date; their 90 days start on their next edit.
