Claude working session — 2026-10-11

1. Our own live-talk voice (Pipecat) is live
- Pushed and deployed earlier today: 7c4a8d2, 8ee417e, 1029150, 471c33d. The voice service is on
  Railway (b4f4c35a), with Cloudflare TURN for WebRTC. The owner made calls and both directions
  work.
- Prompt caching on the voice's Claude Haiku turns works after the fix. Haiku 4.5 caches only
  prompts of 4,096 tokens or more, so the voice prompt now carries the app guide (~6.2k tokens).
  Pipecat counted input tokens twice; `_CountOnce` fixes that. Cached turns cost about
  $0.0008–0.0011 each on Railway.

2. Cheaper hand-offs during calls (owner chose "Both")
- When the voice hands a question to the chat agent (calendar, email, reminders), that look-up
  was a full chat-agent run on the chat's model (~45k tokens of tools and prompt; a cold Sonnet
  write was about $0.115).
- Look-ups now have their own row on the admin's AI models card: "Look-ups during calls"
  (ModelJob "voice", model-choices.ts). It defaults to the server's quick Claude (Haiku, low
  effort, 8k output cap). voice-brain.ts sends `forwardedProps.voiceLookup`, and
  ConversationAgent then picks `modelFor("voice")`. change_ai_models, /api/usage, Usage's models
  line and aiCosts all know the new job.
- The chat agent's fixed instructions now use a 1-hour cache (`ttl: "1h"` on system[0], in
  tanstack-agent.ts). The rest of the prompt keeps the 5-minute cache.

3. The bell, the Feed and the dock (owner's requests)
- The bell showed "353 new". Every email made an update, and nothing ever aged out. The owner
  chose "Only what needs you":
  - Email reaches the bell only when it matches a rule, or when the person picks "Every new
    email". "Important email" is gone; it used to count email with no flag as important.
    watch_inbox takes `notify` ("rules"/"all"), and the agent asks which one the person wants.
  - Updates older than 7 days stop showing, unless their job is still waiting on the person
    (`recentUpdates` in service.ts). Nothing is deleted: jobs re-post their outcome every minute,
    so a deleted update would come back.
  - "Mark all read" is in the Updates sheet (POST /api/notifications/read-all). It can also be
    said: "Mark all my updates read" (tool mark_updates_read). The "Needs you" chip and the order
    come from updateKind.
- Feed: news only from the last 48 hours (`KEEP_HOURS`, feed.ts). Older items are pruned on
  refresh.
- Dock: every bottom-bar icon has a tip on hover (long press on phones) with its name and what's
  there, e.g. "Chat: talk or type to Neddy".
- Help: the updates, email-alerts, feed and admin-models topics are updated. The models shot was
  retaken with the new row; the shot script now mocks it and closes the corner pop-up.
- Design division: visual, usability and copy all signed off (round 2; copy in round 3).

Tests: Biome clean, typecheck, worker tsc, pnpm test 530 pass / 0 fail. New tests:
