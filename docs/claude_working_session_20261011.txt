  - voices.py: 8 hand-picked Inworld voices with our own short descriptions (Ashley, Deborah,

  - Front routes GET /v1/voices and /v1/voices/{id}/sample (a short MP3 hello, made once per

  - Each call takes `openmuse.voice`, and a mid-call `session.update` switches it at once (Pipecat

    process per voice (a few characters of Inworld).
  - "Still checking" now has 5 lines, never the same twice in a row.
  - The log line "Voice call … started (…, voice X)" shows which voice a call used.
