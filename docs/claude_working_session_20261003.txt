An Idea that asked for the same OK four times
- Report: the owner tapped an Idea ("organize your glasses vendor pipeline (YuanFeng Tech, Solos,
  AIAF) into a tracker"). Every approval took three screens (the "Needs you" pop-up's Open, the
  job page's "Review action", then "One last look" scrolled to Approve), and the job asked for the
  same "Create a Google Drive spreadsheet" step four times (approved at 10:17, 10:19, 10:19 and
  10:20 UTC, with one question at 10:18). It then ended by asking to connect Google Sheets, but
  saved that as a finished "report"; its "Explore full result" did nothing.
- Causes:
  - A job starts its work again after each approval, with only `approvalResult` in its saved
    state. The model wrote the spreadsheet again with slightly different contents, and the
    repeat check keyed on the whole step, including the agent's own summary, so each rewrite
    was a new review. Up to four copies of the spreadsheet may be in the owner's Google Drive.
  - finish_task accepted "I need you to connect Google Sheets first: <link>" as the job's result.
  - The report card's "Explore full result" only expands rows; this report had none. Its summary
    was plain text, so the markdown link showed as **…** and couldn't be tapped.
  - Approved steps' outcomes weren't logged, so the logs couldn't say whether they failed.
- Owner's choices (asked): the approvals were the same each time; approve on the job page; one OK
  per job ("Also allow the rest of this job in <app>").
- Fixed (before the design review):
  - engine/job-steps.ts: a job's `done` list (app, tool, title, result) of steps it has done; a
    step is the same when its app, tool and short arguments match (long text and the summary
    don't count). A repeat returns the earlier result without a review. The job prompt says to
    carry on from `done`. Recorded on resume after an approval, when a step was allowed without
    asking, and when the same review had already run.
  - The review key for app steps (jobs and chat) is app + tool + arguments, never the summary.
  - finish_task and a run's last words: asking to connect an app first (a Composio link, or
    "connect … first") is a question: the job waits, the link is clickable.
  - Server log: "Approved step ran: <tool> (<app>)" or "Approved step failed: … <reason>".
  - One OK per job: the Approve card (job page, Activity, chat) and the review sheet offer "Also
    allow the rest of this job in Google Drive (deleting, cancelling and paying still ask)". The
    server keeps it per job (`job-allowances`); that job's later steps in that app go ahead
    without asking, except destructive ones; purchases always ask.
  - Job page: the chat's Approve card replaces "Ready for your review / Review action", so it's
    pop-up Open → Approve (2 taps).
  - Report card: the summary renders as the agent wrote it (links work); "Explore full result"
    shows only when there's more.
  - App names: Google Drive, Google Sheets, Gmail, OneDrive… instead of "Googledrive"; the
    review sheet says "Always allow “Create file from text” in Google Drive" instead of the
    raw slug.
  - Help: "approvals" (the job page, "Also allow the rest of this job") and "always-allowed".
  - Tests: tests/job-steps.test.ts (4): same step despite new contents; key without summary;
    connect-first is a question; the rest-of-job allowance is per job and app and needs the
    right hash.
- Browser check (scratchpad approve/review.mjs, 416 light and 390 dark): pop-up Open → job page
  with Approve & run and the checkbox; Approve sends restOfJob: true; the report's link is
  clickable and has no Explore button; no page errors.
- Checks: Biome clean, typecheck root/app/worker, 485 tests pass.
- Told the owner how to get the tracker now: ask in the chat for a spreadsheet saved in Files
  (no Google needed), or connect Google Sheets and ask again; and check Drive for copies of
  "Wearable Glasses Vendor Pipeline".
