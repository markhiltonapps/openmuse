WHY
---
Owner: "I want to be able to have a conversation with this AI in almost real time… on my phone,
in my car using my Android audio and also on my computer… very fast… interrupt." Plan in the
scratchpad (voice-live/plan.md).

Owner's decisions (asked with a picker, three rounds):
- Where: phone, car and computer.
- Engine: OpenAI's gpt-live-1 for the voice, with Claude as the brain (from slice 1).
- Car: both a phone number to call and the app over Bluetooth. Later, a wearable microphone
  with a wake word through an Android/iOS app, so the audio source has to be pluggable.
- Limits: no daily limit for now, but count minutes per person.
- Barge-in: the person can cut Neddy off by talking.
- Spoken "yes" in the app only (never on the phone line), for reminders and notes, the owner's

- Key: the owner's existing OpenAI key, in the Railway variable OPENAI_VOICE_API_KEY. The owner
  added it on Railway themselves.

- Pushes: slice by slice.

WHAT CHANGED
------------
1. Server (new apps/server/src/voice-live.ts; app.ts, config.ts, index.ts, usage.ts).
   - POST /api/voice/live takes the browser's WebRTC offer, starts a gpt-live-1 session with
     Neddy's name, tone, the time, the "about you" notes and memories, and returns only the
     answer. The key stays on the server.
   - The server attaches to the session's sideband socket. That gives it the transcript of both
     sides, OpenAI's running minutes, and look-up requests. Look-ups get a polite "not yet, type
     it in the chat" until slice 1.
   - One call per person: a new call ends the old one.
   - Limits and hang-ups:
     - calls stop after 60 minutes;
     - after 90 s with no words either way, the server hangs up (VOICE_IDLE_SECONDS);
     - a lost sideband reattaches once;
     - a server restart ends calls cleanly and saves them.
   - Each call is saved (the voice-sessions store: turns, seconds, why it ended).
     GET /api/voice/live/recent lists the latest calls.
   - Usage meter: a new "voice" kind with seconds, priced per minute (5¢ for gpt-live-1;
     VOICE_PRICES can override it). Usage shows "Live voice" minutes, and the People list shows
     minutes per person.
   - Only admins (the owner) get it for now. Other people see no headset.
   - Settings: OPENAI_VOICE_API_KEY, VOICE_LIVE_MODEL, VOICE_LIVE_VOICE (default marin),
     VOICE_IDLE_SECONDS.
2. App (new apps/mobile/src/live-voice.ts and live-talk-ui.tsx; chat.tsx, ui.tsx, details.tsx,
   workspace.tsx, usage-ui.tsx, voice.ts).
   - The headset button in the chat composer (blue, tip "Talk live with Neddy") opens "Talking
     with Neddy".
   - The sheet shows Neddy's avatar, the mouth moving with the voice, and a status: Connecting… /
     Listening… / Neddy is talking, just speak to cut in / Muted.
   - Live captions sit in a fixed box, so nothing jumps.
   - Mute and End are in a footer that stays on screen. After a call ends: Talk again and Close,
     with a plain reason (connection dropped, quiet, an hour, started elsewhere, restart).
   - Echo cancellation is on. If the chosen microphone isn't there, the default one is used. The
     screen is kept awake during a call.
   - Microphone errors are named in plain words: blocked, none found, in use, or no connection.
   - Screen readers: a hidden status line is read only on real changes. Focus moves to the new
     buttons when they swap, skipping a disabled Mute.
   - Short screens (a phone on its side in a car mount) get a smaller avatar, or none.
   - Below 360 px wide, the composer shows only the headset, not the old voice button.
