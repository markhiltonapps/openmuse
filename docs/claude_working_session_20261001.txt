==========================================================================================

WHY
---
The owner ran two real jobs ("compare robot vacuums…", "least expensive AI glasses with a
waveguide") and found the job page confusing: a "Plan" of four steps that never changed, the
agent's running commentary shown as a question ("Needs your answer"), a PDF report with raw table
pipes, raw links and "?" for stars, and New job buried in the menu with unexplained job types.
Owner's words: if what you show while it works isn't visually pleasing, show nothing; tell me it's
running, and when it's done put a button in my face. Also asked for an animation (gears or Neddy)
that keeps showing work is happening, on any screen.

Owner's decisions (asked with a picker): Neddy busy at work as the animation; one tidy "now doing"
line from a fixed list; New job as BOTH a + in the top bar and a floating button; remove the job
types and keep one box, with the guided ones behind More options.

WHAT CHANGED
------------
1. Jobs end cleanly (server, apps/server/src/engine/model.ts + new engine/job-words.ts).
   - The agent's text is tracked per message; only its words after the last tool call count.
   - A run that stops without finish_task/ask_user: an answer is taken as the result (job Done,
     final report saved), a question asks only that question, and a stop part-way ("Let me check
     the price:") asks "I stopped before finishing. Tell me what to do next…". An offer after a
     full answer ("Would you like a PDF?") is dropped. This fixes the glasses job's screen.
   - "Now doing" line: each tool call sets task.state.now / nowKind from a fixed list
     ("Searching the web", "Looking at amazon.com", "Using amazon.com", "Making your PDF",
     "Checking your calendar"…). Never the model's own words.
2. PDF and Word documents (packages/integrations/src/compose.ts).
   - Markdown tables become real tables (shaded header, row lines, wrapped cells, header repeated
     on a new page; short columns keep their width; number columns right-aligned).
   - [text](url) and bare https:// links are blue, underlined and clickable (one link area each).
   - ★ is drawn as a shape; ⭐ ✅ ❌ map to symbols; symbol fonts cover → ≈ ✓ etc.
   - Accent rule under the title, page numbers "1 of 3" on longer documents.
   - create_document's description tells the agent tables and links work.
3. Job page (apps/mobile/src/agent-ui.tsx TaskDetail, new apps/mobile/src/job-working-ui.tsx).
   - Running/queued: one card. Neddy (the looping clip) with an activity prop inside a turning
     ring, "Working on it" / "Getting started", the now-doing line, "Started 4 min ago", "You can
     leave this page…", and Stop (asks "Stop this job? [Stop it] [Keep going]").
   - Hidden while running: the filler plan (a real plan the agent made shows in Details), browser
     previews (shown only when the job needs the person), half-made files, Where I looked.
   - Paused card (Resume / Stop job), Stopped card, question card says "I’ll carry on once you
     answer." with "Type your answer…" / "Send answer". AI cost moved into Details.
   - Screen readers hear each change (Done, Stopped, Paused…) and focus moves to the new card's
     heading when the pressed button has gone. Long titles clip to 3 lines.
   - Job cards in Activity/Chat/Goals no longer show "0/4 steps"; a running card shows its
     now-doing line.
4. New job everywhere (apps/mobile/App.tsx).
   - + button next to the bell on every screen; floating "+ New job" above the bottom bar on every
     screen but Chat (a round + under 360px; hidden while Back to chat or voice mode shows).
   - New job sheet: one box "What would you like done?", "Say it instead", More options (Anything /
     Fill in a PDF from an email / Sum up spending from a bank file (CSV)), "Start job".
   - Menu entry, app guide and take-me-there catalogue renamed to New job; task → job wording on
     these screens and in the server's job errors.
   - Header: Neddy's status line says what the job is doing, in blue; "Needs your answer" /
     "Ready to review" in dark text; a queued job shows "Getting started…". Tapping Neddy opens
     the job.
5. Open tabs pick up new versions (apps/mobile/src/web-app.ts watchForUpdates + App NewVersion).
   The cause of the owner seeing the old job page: an already-open tab keeps running the old
   bundle. Now, coming back to the tab after 30s reloads when nothing is typed and nothing is open;
   otherwise (and every 15 minutes) a pill offers "Reload", warning if typed text would be lost.
