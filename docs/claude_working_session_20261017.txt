Meeting recorder (owner, Oct 17: "put the recording capability on the mobile device"; the desktop
helper for Zoom/Webex/Meet audio is set aside). Mostly in-person meetings of about an hour.
Owner's choices: start from Home, the chat and by voice; keep audio 30 days, then words only;
summary, decisions and action items afterwards; everyone gets 10 hours a month.

What changed
- Server (apps/server/src/meetings.ts, new):
  - Recording arrives in 30-second pieces per part (PUT /api/meetings/:id/chunks/:part/:n).
    A phone lock ends a part; the next part starts on return. Each part is joined and sent
    to Soniox's async API (stt-async-v5, speakers on). Later parts' speakers are kept apart
    ("2:1").
  - The "simple" model writes a title, summary, decisions and action items. Naming a speaker
    ("Speaker 2 was Dana") changes the words, the action items and what search finds.
  - Audio is kept 30 days (signed links, Range support). Words and notes stay until deleted.
  - Each person gets MEETING_MONTHLY_HOURS (default 10) a month, and a part runs 4 hours at most.
    Usage kind "meetings" costs $0.10 an hour.
  - Recordings left open close themselves after 45 minutes without a piece, and are written
    up. On failure, a notification and Try again. Only one write-up runs at a time per
    meeting (a lease plus a map of running ones).
  - Routes:
    - GET/POST /api/meetings
    - PUT .../chunks
    - POST .../finish, /discard, /retry
    - GET/PATCH/DELETE /api/meetings/:id
    - GET .../audio/:part
  - The notification opens /?meeting=<id>.
- Agent:
  - The chat and calls get a "meetings" tool (start, list, read, search, name_speakers,
    rename). Background jobs can list, read and search.
  - Recent meetings are in the chat's context.
  - Saying "Record a meeting" on a call shows a card that ends the call and starts recording.
- App:
  - The recorder (meeting-recorder.ts) uses MediaRecorder at 32 kbps Opus (MP4 on Safari),
    keeps the screen awake and shows a level meter. Pieces upload in order with backoff.
  - A pause is a lock, a mute of 3 s or more, or the mic ending; "Carry on" resumes.
  - Plain messages when:
    - the server closed the recording;
    - Stop couldn't reach the server;
    - Throw away was refused or couldn't reach the server.
  - The recorder layer (MeetingRecorderProvider) sits above the call.
  - A red bar shows on every screen and sheet while recording.
  - Entry points:
    - a round Record button on Home (Talk and Type made room for it on narrow phones);
    - "Record a meeting" in the chat's + menu;
    - Files › Meetings.
  - The meeting page shows the summary, decisions, action items with Make reminders, the
    audio while it's kept, and every word with who said it (tap a name to say who it is).
    It also has Rename, Ask about it and Delete.
  - Usage shows "Recorded meetings" in minutes.
- Help:
  - New topic "record-meeting" (Files group, place "meetings"), with a "meetings" screenshot.
  - New admin topic "admin-meetings".
  - The app guide mentions Meetings.
- Setup: .env.example and the README have SONIOX_API_KEY, MEETING_MONTHLY_HOURS and
  SONIOX_API_BASE_URL.

Prompt caching fix (owner: "start the caching fix"): the per-turn context (the time; on a call,
what's on screen) sat in the system prompt before the messages, so the conversation never
matched the cache between turns (chat: "read 66150 (41133 cached)" every turn; a hand-off:
"read 38845 (0 cached) · $0.0488"). Now (engine/tanstack-agent.ts):
- the steady context (who you are, memories, people, meetings, summaries, VOICE_RULES) is a
  second system block with a 5-minute cache mark;
- the volatile context (`VOLATILE_CONTEXT`: the time, a call's screen) and the app state go in
  a text block after the person's words in their latest message (`withTail`), and their words
  carry a cache mark, so the next turn's lookup finds the conversation up to them;
- the top-level cache mark stays for the rest of a reply (tool calls and results).
  Four breakpoints in all (the API's limit). The runtime's message copies aren't changed.
Measured after the deploy (20:29–20:30Z, Railway's "Model call: chat" lines): the first message
read 70,673 (0 cached, $0.177, the cache write); the next step and the next message read
~71,300 with ~70,600 cached, $0.016–0.019 each. Before: ~66,000 read, 41,000 cached, ~$0.075
each. So follow-up messages cost about a quarter of what they did; the first message after a
quiet spell (5 min for the conversation, 1 h for the instructions) still pays the write. Tests: prompt-caching.test.ts checks the request shape
and the two-turn match.

Per-minute cost, round 2 (owner, Oct 18: "stop listening during long silence, hang up after 30
seconds"): the quiet hang-up on our own voice is now 30 s (VOICE_PIPECAT_IDLE_SECONDS default,
config.ts; README, .env.example and the admin Help topic say so). "Stop listening" was looked
into and not built: Soniox bills the open stream's whole duration (its docs: billed even while
paused; a keepalive only keeps the socket open), so saving anything means closing the stream in
silences and reopening it on speech with a buffered pre-roll. Silence costs only Soniox's
0.2¢/min (nothing speaks or thinks), so the safe version (close after ~8 s of quiet) saves
~0.01–0.03¢/min and the aggressive one (open only while the person speaks) ~0.1¢/min with a
reconnect at every turn; Soniox is blocked from the sandbox, so it couldn't be tested here. The
owner was told and asked; the 30 s hang-up alone saves ~0.05¢ per call.
