What was built (uncommitted until the Design division signs off, then a local commit; no push
until the owner says "push it")
1. Plans (packages/domain/src/plans.ts, shared by server, app and tests)
   - Free (5 call minutes, 100 messages, 30 minutes of meetings, 1 job; about $1.81 a month at the
     ceilings), Starter, Neddy, Neddy Max (exactly 5x Neddy), plus an internal "early" plan.
   - EXTRAS packs: 30 call minutes $3.60, 50 messages $4, 1 meeting hour 80¢, 1 job $1.50.
   - limitText(): the words said at a limit (kind, when it comes back, where to write). Before
     billing it says "For more room, email hello@neatoventures.com and a person will help"; with
     billing=true it names extras and the next plan. It still starts "You’ve used this month’s",
     which the call screen's isLiveLimit relies on.
2. Server (apps/server/src/plans.ts, wired in app.ts)
   - A person's plan is "system"/"plans"/<owner>. No plan means early access: the old limits
     (VOICE_MONTHLY_MINUTES 200, MEETING_MONTHLY_HOURS 10), messages and jobs not counted, so
     nothing changed for testers. People invited from now on get Free (accounts.invite).
   - Counts per UTC month (same month as the usage meter): typed messages and handed-off jobs, kept
     in "plan-counts" with the last 60 ids so a retry isn't counted twice; one at a time per
     person. Extras are added by the admin per month ("plan-extras").
   - Messages: ConversationAgent.run counts a typed message before the model; past the limit it
     replies with a kind note and no model call. Live-call hand-offs (threadId voice-…), saved
     calls and voice look-ups are not counted. A counting failure never blocks a chat.
   - Jobs: createTask counts a job the person hands off (input.handedOff) and refuses past the limit
     (429, kind text). Routine runs and watches are NOT counted yet (risk noted in pricing_math.md).
   - Call minutes and meeting hours: voiceAllowance and Meetings.allowance now read the person's plan.
   - Routes: GET /api/plan (own), POST /api/accounts/:id/plan, POST /api/accounts/:id/extras (admin);
     GET /api/accounts includes each person's plan. Tools: my_plan (anyone), set_plan (admin, by voice
     or chat: "Put Dana on Starter", "Add 50 messages for Dana").
3. App: "Your plan" block at the top of Usage (usage-ui.tsx), plan and extras buttons per invited person
   in People (account-ui.tsx). Help: new "plan" topic, admin-people updated. No screenshots changed
   (those topics have none).
4. Website: pricing.html has a Free band above the three paid cards, a Free row in the phone glance card,
   an FAQ "Is there a free plan?" and "Do you train on my chats?" (4 cards, 2 columns), counts copy
   ("Messages have a ceiling"), meta descriptions. compare.html's price cell says "Free, then $10, $20 or
   $100". docs/pricing_math.md has the Free plan's cost.
5. Tests: tests/plans.test.ts (10 tests: plan numbers, early access unchanged, counting once, races,
   month rollover, extras, limit text, the kind note instead of a model call, routes and tools, new
   invitees start on Free). A real bug found by the tests: nextPlan("early") offered Free.

Known gaps and risks (for the owner)
- Stripe billing, self-service plan changes/cancel and buying extras in the app are not built. Until then
  the admin sets plans and adds extras by hand; people are told to email.
- Routine runs and watches aren't counted as jobs; a Free person with routines can cost more than $1.81.
- Providers: "we don't train on your chats" is Neato's own promise. Check each provider's terms
  (Anthropic API, OpenAI voice, OpenRouter models, Soniox, Inworld, CopilotKit) and OpenRouter's data
  policy setting before relying on it.
- Month boundaries: counts use the UTC month (like the usage meter); the "comes back on" date uses the
  person's time zone, so around midnight on the 1st the two can differ by a few hours.
- Idea acceptance creates a goal before the job; if the job is refused past the limit the goal remains.
