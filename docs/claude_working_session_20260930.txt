Health space (Spaces → Health)
- Everyone gets one (made the first time Spaces loads; if they remove it, "Start a health
  space" brings it back with their log).
- Overview: today's calories, protein and exercise against their targets; a "By day" bar chart
  for any week (Previous / Next week), switchable between calories, protein and exercise, with
  "Show the numbers" for a table of the same; "Week by week" (the last 8 weeks as a table);
  Workouts to start again.
- Food log: every meal as a table, per day, for today, this week or 30 days, with totals; Change
  fixes a meal.
- Food plan: the Family space's planned dinners next to what was eaten, week by week.
- Playbook: goals, food rules, targets (calories, protein, weekly exercise), "Ask me what I ate"
  (meal check-ins and their times), and the weekly check-in.
- Goals › Health, Feed's "View food log" and the "Log a meal" sheet all lead here.
- Server: GET /api/spaces/:id/health?week= (apps/server/src/health.ts), and a get_food_log tool
  so Neddy can answer "what have I eaten this week?".
- Neddy now has a map of the app (apps/server/src/app-guide.ts), so when someone asks where to
  find something it gives the real path, like "Spaces → Health → Food log".
