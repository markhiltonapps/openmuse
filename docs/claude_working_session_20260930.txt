Claude working session — 2026-09-30
===================================

WHAT CHANGED
------------
You asked for items 2–4 from last night's list, tips wherever they help, a place to see your
health data (meals eaten, nutrition charts, the food plan, workouts), and tables and charts for
every other kind of data the app keeps ("everything at once").

Bottom bar (items 2–4)
- Labels under every tab ("Files" for Files & media), a people icon (UsersRound) for Spaces,
  and a blue pill behind the selected tab's icon. All 8 tabs stay on small phones (labels are
  10px below 360px wide).
- The family board: when it's empty, the button reads "Plan this week" and plans this week
  (it used to plan next week).

Tips (hover, keyboard, and press and hold on phones)
- New: apps/mobile/src/tips.ts, TipLayer.web.tsx, TipLayer.native.tsx.
- A tip shows when a mouse rests on a control (350ms), when the keyboard moves to it, and on a
  touch screen when a finger holds it (700ms). The chat's Send and mic buttons don't have the
  hold, so a slow press still sends.
- An ⓘ next to a term opens its tip on a tap; it stays until the next tap, a scroll or Escape.
- On the web the tip is drawn above everything, sheets included. Escape closes only the tip,
  not the sheet under it. A mouse can move onto a tip to read it.
- Tips are on icon-only buttons across the app, on every bar and point in the charts, and ⓘ
  tips explain terms (Needs you, Weekly digest, Saved questions, Week by week, and others).

Health space (Spaces → Health)
- Everyone gets one (made the first time Spaces loads; if they remove it, "Start a health
  space" brings it back with their log).
- Overview: today's calories, protein and exercise against their targets; a "By day" bar chart
  for any week (Previous / Next week), switchable between calories, protein and exercise, with
  "Show the numbers" for a table of the same; "Week by week" (the last 8 weeks as a table);
  Workouts to start again.
- Food log: every meal as a table, per day, for today, this week or 30 days, with totals; Change
  fixes a meal.
- Food plan: the Family space's planned dinners next to what was eaten, week by week.
- Playbook: goals, food rules, targets (calories, protein, weekly exercise), "Ask me what I ate"
  (meal check-ins and their times), and the weekly check-in.
- Goals › Health, Feed's "View food log" and the "Log a meal" sheet all lead here.
- Server: GET /api/spaces/:id/health?week= (apps/server/src/health.ts), and a get_food_log tool
  so Neddy can answer "what have I eaten this week?".
- Neddy now has a map of the app (apps/server/src/app-guide.ts), so when someone asks where to
  find something it gives the real path, like "Spaces → Health → Food log".

Views for every other data gap (built by two helpers, reviewed with the rest)
- Plans & bookings (Feed → "See all plans & bookings"): upcoming and past, mark done or
  cancelled, with Undo.
- Reminders (Feed → "See all reminders"): upcoming, and those sent in the last 7 days.
- Saved by Neddy (Files): every report, comparison, plan and finance tracker Neddy saved.
- Money (Apps → Money): this month's purchases, spending by month (chart), AI usage by month.
- Tracking (Goals): a price chart for a price watch, and every check a watch made.
- Social media › Results: past posts, including the ones that didn't go out and why.
- Family › Our weeks: chore stars by week.

Shared pieces
- apps/mobile/src/charts.tsx: bar chart, table (it folds a column under the first one on narrow
  phones), stat tiles, meters, and a choice control that works with the arrow keys.
- apps/mobile/src/charts-extra.tsx: the line chart for price watches.

Design division
- Three rounds with the UI Designer (visual), UX Researcher (usability) and copy reviewers.
  All three approved. Among the fixes: nothing cut off or split mid-word at 320px, tables
  with names for screen readers, keyboard focus that lands in the right place after Mark done
  or Undo, units in the numbers tables, meal check-ins called the same thing everywhere.
