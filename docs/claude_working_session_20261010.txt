Owner's question: on opening the app, the chat "fills up" for a few seconds and looks
unfinished; will a long main chat cause problems?

What we found
- The app's server answers in about a second; the wait is the chat service (CopilotKit
  Intelligence) replaying the whole chat to the browser piece by piece, straight from its cloud
  (not in Railway's logs), with the app redrawing every message for every piece, showing the
  new-chat welcome screen meanwhile, and old cards each fetching data.
- Measured (new log line "Main chat size …", once an hour per person): the owner's main chat was
  505 messages (59 theirs, 139 replies, 196 tool results), 1.8 MB, already summarized; backup
  copy 400. Neddy's own context stays small (summaries), but every open downloads and every send
  uploads the whole chat; sends would fail at the 12 MB body limit (~350 more of their
  messages at that pace) and the backup copy stops at 5,000 messages.

Owner's decisions
- Both fixes. Main chat turns a new part by size (~1 MB); earlier parts via a button at the top.

Built (committed locally, not pushed)
- Server, apps/server/src/main-pages.ts:
  - The archive save (the app sends the whole chat after each turn) and the size log mark the main
    chat once it passes 1 MB; the next GET /api/main-thread saves the finished part whole
    ("main-pages"), starts a fresh CopilotKit thread and returns {pages}.
  - A summary of everything up to the end of the part is written in the background (the chat's own
    summary + its unsummarized tail + the previous part's summary; falls back to the newest part
    that has one).
  - The chat turn gets that summary ("the main chat's older parts"), and search_earlier_chat
    searches every earlier part, newest first, one at a time.
  - GET /api/main-thread/pages/:id returns a part (deleted messages left out).
  - Resetting the main chat deletes every part.
  - A call's snapshot includes the end of the last part when the current one is short.
- App:
  - chat-rows.tsx: MessageRow (each message drawn again only when its words, cards or buttons
    change; compares a text signature since CopilotKit may update messages in place), SceneLabel,
    EarlierDivider, and this device's copy of each chat's last ~30 messages (≤400 KB, 5 most recent
    chats; dropped for earlier parts and on sign-out).
  - chat.tsx:
    - Opens on the device copy (read-only) or a "Loading your conversation…" pill while the
      history replays; the replay no longer counts as replying (no "Thinking…" bubble or stop
      button).
    - Only the latest 40 messages are drawn; "Show earlier messages" adds 40 (and, once all are
      drawn, loads the previous part), keeping the messages on screen in place.
    - "Continued Oct 8" between parts; "Start of your conversation" when nothing is older.
    - A short new part waits for the end of the previous one, so they appear together.
    - An always-mounted hidden status line announces loading and each tap.
  - assistant-response.tsx: memoized; a fixed plugins list stops every reply being re-parsed on
    each redraw.
  - Sandbox and approval cards from history no longer each reload the whole workspace.
  - threads.tsx/chats-ui.tsx: earlier parts never show as separate chats.
- Help: topic "past-chats" explains Show earlier messages and the parts ("Continued Oct 8").

Activity tidy-up: clearing a pile of ~100 jobs
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Owner: "I'm starting to get a pile up of activities … almost a hundred. There should be a way to
manage that." (Screenshot: the Activity jobs list, recurring "Outlook Sent & Inbox Check-In · Oct 9"
runs, 9+ on the tab.) Choices: archive (not delete); Clear finished; Select several; tidy up
automatically (7 days); Let go for unfinished jobs they don't want.

What changed
- Server (engine/service.ts, routes.ts):
  - Jobs and receipts get `archivedAt` (kept, still open from the chat and Spaces) and
    `restoredAt` (the weekly tidy counts a restored item as new).
  - POST /api/agent/tasks/archive {ids?, stop?, routinesOff?}: no ids = every finished job plus
    every decided receipt; with ids, unfinished ones only with stop (stopped first: nothing more
    is done or sent, a waiting approval is turned down). Optional routinesOff turns routines off
    in the same tidy.
  - POST /tasks/restore, POST /tasks/:id/let-go, POST /tasks/undo (the last tidy is saved per
    person; Undo brings back exactly that batch, stopped jobs stay stopped, routines turned back on).
  - Maintenance archives finished jobs and decided receipts after 7 days (hourly; not on the
    sample server, whose dates are fixed in the past).
  - Routine runs now carry input.routineId (older ones match "<routine> · <day>").
  - Stopping a job whose record has no lease field (never picked up) no longer fails with "This
    job just changed".
- Chat and calls: tool tidy_activity (clear_finished, archive, let_go, let_go_waiting, restore,
  undo, turn_off_routine), matching jobs by name; asks which when several match; never stops a
  job unless asked; offers to turn off a routine whose run was stopped.
- App (agent-ui.tsx ActivityJobs, TaskCard):
  - Filters All / In progress / Finished / Archived; Clear finished and Select on the right.
  - Each job has Archive (finished), Let go (unfinished, with a confirm naming what happens; for a
    routine's run, "Let go and turn off the routine") or Restore (Archived).
  - A tidied job leaves a line in its place with Undo; focus moves there.
  - Select mode: checkboxes, Select all, Archive / Stop and archive / Restore, Cancel; a
    fixed-height line says how many are selected and what will happen.
  - The job page has Archive / Restore to Activity. Archived receipts are hidden from Reviews &
    receipts.
- Help: new topic "Clear out Activity" (tidy-activity); Activity topic updated, its shot gets a
  mark on Clear finished; app guide line.
