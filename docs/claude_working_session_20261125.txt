## Chat screen "jammed up" (owner screenshots: the lunch question card, "3 things need you", badge said 4)
Reproduced locally: on a phone the conversation got about 35% of the screen. The lunch card had its own mic, its own text box and
send, a "Same as yesterday" row, two buttons and a footnote, all above the real message box; the docked notice stayed until acted on.

What changed (apps/mobile/src/meal-checkins-ui.tsx, chat.tsx, update-toasts.tsx, heads-ups-ui.tsx; apps/server/src/meal-checkins.ts):
- The meal check-in is one 52px line: fork icon, the question (short form "What was lunch?" where room is tight), Answer, and a chevron.
  Answer starts the message box's own dictation inside the tap with "Log my lunch: " already in the box (the keyboard stays down);
  without speech input it puts the cursor there. One place to type or talk. While it listens, a "Listening for lunch…" line with Stop
  takes the line's place; the strip comes back if the words are cleared, or once the reply to the sent answer is in (60 s fallback).
  Send stays off while the box holds only "Log my lunch:". The untouched prefix is cleared on blur (not when focus only moved to the mic
  or Stop) and when listening ends with no words. A refused microphone keeps the words, focuses the box, and says
  "Allow microphone access… Or type your answer here." in its own notice (mic errors no longer show the reply's "Retry response").
- Quick answers (Same again / Skipped / Later): on a phone they open ABOVE the line, so the line never moves under a thumb, one row,
  44px; at 600px+ they sit in the line itself (one tap). Screen-reader names start with the visible words ("Later: ask again in an hour").
  "Yesterday: …" line shows only on windows 760px+ tall. Beside a heads-up the line takes the card colour so the heads-up keeps the tint.
- The "N things need you" / update notice in the chat folds on the person's next action instead of staying (or an 8 s timer, which was
  tried and rejected: it moved the conversation under a thumb and could pull the notice away from a screen reader): sending, talking
  (voice mode, a spoken send, the call button), Answer or the chevron, a finger lifted after dragging the list, or the wheel at rest
  (only when the list can scroll). Reminders and a finished handed-off job still stay. Outside the chat, updates float and slide away as before.
- Counts: the notice's number is the badge's (waitingOnYou), and "See them" goes to Home's Needs you list; "1 thing still needs you"
  when several came in and one still waits. ✕ for these says "Close. They’re still under Needs you on Home." Curly apostrophes throughout.
- New chat tool skip_meal_checkin (meal, skipped|later) so "I skipped lunch" / "ask me about lunch later" work by voice; Later is always
  an hour and the time comes back already in the person's clock.
- Heads-up stack lost its extra 12px bottom margin (gaps are an even 10).
Measured (Playwright, mocked lunch + breakfast check-ins and 3 waiting jobs): 390x844 answer area ~35% before; ~58% with the notice,
~65% after it folds; 1280x800 and 1100x640 checked, 1913x911 with a heads-up, dark mode, touch drag (notice stays while the finger is

"I skipped lunch." / "Ask me about lunch later." as things to say; the "updates" topic's notice paragraph rewritten. headsups picture
retaken (spacing). Website: nothing on neddy.html, pricing or compare shows the check-in or the notice, so no change.

## Composer follow-ups (owner: "do the two composer follow-ups too")
- Listening shows on the message box itself: a 2px blue border, a light blue tint and the placeholder "Listening…" while the mic
  is on (not in voice mode, which has its own bar).
- Once the words run past one line beside the buttons, the box takes its own row above them (as on a narrow phone) and stays there
  until it's empty, so a dictated or long typed message isn't squeezed into a third of the width.
- Found and fixed while testing: the box was rendered in a different parent when the layout switched, so React rebuilt it mid-typing
  (focus lost, keys dropped: Playwright's typed sentence came out as "C"). The composer now keeps the box in one place in the tree
  (container row/column switch, the + button rendered in either spot). This also fixes the same loss at the existing narrow-phone switch.
- Help: the "mic" line in the chat topic says the box turns blue and shows Listening…, and what you said lands in the box to check.
- Checked at 390x844 and 1280x800: resting, typing a long sentence (all of it kept), listening, heard words. Tests 915/915, tsc clean,
  biome no errors.

## Research: how Meta Muse feels so smart (owner asked for a deep research pass)
- Report: docs/research/How Meta Muse feels so smart.md (notes from four researchers beside it). Headline: Muse runs a mid-tier
  model (Muse Spark 1.3) inside a lot of machinery: a cloud computer per person, hourly memory and relationship passes, a
  produce → verify → publish chain for unprompted notices (~1 a day), a "tracking" store for plans, a warm persona that owns mistakes,
  and decision-first answers. The airport/gate example isn't published anywhere checkable; the likely mechanism is inferred.
- Ten ranked builds for Neddy in the report (capture plans from chat, read emails and a 60-day backfill first; a chat-model bake-off
  incl. Haiku 5.5 and Sonnet 5.5; fewer tools per turn; a blind side-by-side against Muse; saved lessons from corrections; ...).
