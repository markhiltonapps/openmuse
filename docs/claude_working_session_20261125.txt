## Chat screen "jammed up" (owner screenshots: the lunch question card, "3 things need you", badge said 4)

send, a "Same as yesterday" row, two buttons and a footnote, all above the real message box; the docked notice stayed until acted on.

What changed (apps/mobile/src/meal-checkins-ui.tsx, chat.tsx, update-toasts.tsx, heads-ups-ui.tsx; apps/server/src/meal-checkins.ts):
- The meal check-in is one 52px line: fork icon, the question (short form "What was lunch?" where room is tight), Answer, and a chevron.
  Answer starts the message box's own dictation inside the tap with "Log my lunch: " already in the box (the keyboard stays down);
  without speech input it puts the cursor there. One place to type or talk. While it listens, a "Listening for lunch…" line with Stop
  takes the line's place; the strip comes back if the words are cleared, or once the reply to the sent answer is in (60 s fallback).
  Send stays off while the box holds only "Log my lunch:". The untouched prefix is cleared on blur (not when focus only moved to the mic
  or Stop) and when listening ends with no words. A refused microphone keeps the words, focuses the box, and says
  "Allow microphone access… Or type your answer here." in its own notice (mic errors no longer show the reply's "Retry response").
- Quick answers (Same again / Skipped / Later): on a phone they open ABOVE the line, so the line never moves under a thumb, one row,
  44px; at 600px+ they sit in the line itself (one tap). Screen-reader names start with the visible words ("Later: ask again in an hour").
  "Yesterday: …" line shows only on windows 760px+ tall. Beside a heads-up the line takes the card colour so the heads-up keeps the tint.
- The "N things need you" / update notice in the chat folds on the person's next action instead of staying (or an 8 s timer, which was
  tried and rejected: it moved the conversation under a thumb and could pull the notice away from a screen reader): sending, talking
  (voice mode, a spoken send, the call button), Answer or the chevron, a finger lifted after dragging the list, or the wheel at rest
  (only when the list can scroll). Reminders and a finished handed-off job still stay. Outside the chat, updates float and slide away as before.
- Counts: the notice's number is the badge's (waitingOnYou), and "See them" goes to Home's Needs you list; "1 thing still needs you"
  when several came in and one still waits. ✕ for these says "Close. They’re still under Needs you on Home." Curly apostrophes throughout.
- New chat tool skip_meal_checkin (meal, skipped|later) so "I skipped lunch" / "ask me about lunch later" work by voice; Later is always
  an hour and the time comes back already in the person's clock.
- Heads-up stack lost its extra 12px bottom margin (gaps are an even 10).

## Research: how Meta Muse feels so smart (owner asked for a deep research pass)
- Report: docs/research/How Meta Muse feels so smart.md (notes from four researchers beside it). Headline: Muse runs a mid-tier
  model (Muse Spark 1.3) inside a lot of machinery: a cloud computer per person, hourly memory and relationship passes, a
  produce → verify → publish chain for unprompted notices (~1 a day), a "tracking" store for plans, a warm persona that owns mistakes,
  and decision-first answers. The airport/gate example isn't published anywhere checkable; the likely mechanism is inferred.
- Ten ranked builds for Neddy in the report (capture plans from chat, read emails and a 60-day backfill first; a chat-model bake-off
  incl. Haiku 5.5 and Sonnet 5.5; fewer tools per turn; a blind side-by-side against Muse; saved lessons from corrections; ...).
  Nothing built yet: waiting on the owner's pick.

## #1 from the research: capture everywhere (owner: "push it and start #1")
Pushed e8744ce + aa436c2 first (no live call in Railway's logs).
Before: plans were only picked up from NEW mail arriving through an inbox watch, or when the chat model chose to call
track_commitment. An email the person asked about in chat, a plan said in passing or on a call, and bookings made before
Neddy was set up were never tracked, so nothing could speak up before them (the owner's trip).
Now (apps/server/src/commitments.ts, engine/service.ts, email-views.ts, apps.ts, engine/conversation.ts, voice-live.ts, turn-coach.ts):
- Every Gmail/Outlook email the chat, a job or a call hand-off reads goes through EmailViews.onRead → captureFromEmails: only
  booking-like ones (looksLikeConfirmation), at most 8 a read, one person at a time in a queue, each email checked ONCE
  (`email-checked`), with the full body (up to 2,500 chars, redacted), so gate and terminal get through. Past bookings aren't added.
  The tool result tells the agent these are tracked on their own, so it doesn't add them twice.
- Plans the person says: plain code (`mentionsPlan`: a plan word + a day/time; questions and requests like "schedule…",
  "can you…" don't count) adds a turn-coach note asking for track_commitment; if the turn ends without track/update_commitment,
  one small call (`commitmentsFromWordsPrompt`, at most 3 plans) tracks them in the background. Call hand-offs are skipped:
  at the end of every call (not rehearsals) the person's own words go through the same check.
- Once per person with Gmail connected: a 60-day look back (GMAIL_FETCH_EMAILS, booking words in the subject, 30 emails),
  run from the daily-check loop, tried at most every 6 hours, marked done only when it ran. Outlook's look back isn't built
  (its search arguments aren't verified); Outlook mail is still caught when read or when inbox alerts are on.
- The same plan caught twice (said, then its email; different titles like "Flight to Tokyo" / "Flight to Tokyo (JAL 61)")
  is one plan: same kind, same day, one title inside the other. The email's time replaces a date alone.
- Cost: one small background call per new booking-like email or per plan-like message the model didn't track (< 0.5¢ each).
- Help: Plans & bookings topic (how plans are picked up, the 60-day Gmail look back, Outlook's limit, a "what are you keeping
  an eye on" phrase). Website: the bookings paragraph on neddy.html; looked at 390 and 1280.
- Tests: tests/capture.test.ts (8) + a voice-live check; pnpm test 923/923; tsc clean; biome no errors.
- Untested live: Composio's GMAIL_FETCH_EMAILS answer shape for the look back (parsed with the same emailsIn the chat uses);
  check Railway for "Looked back through 60 days of Gmail: N plan(s) tracked" and "Plans caught from what was said: N".

## 1, 2 and 9: privacy you can prove, travel mode, calm by design (owner: "do 1, 2 and 9")

### 1. Privacy you can prove (apps/server/src/privacy.ts, apps/mobile/src/data-ui.tsx)
- "Forget everything about X": `Forgetting.find` looks through memories, About you facts, people, plans, saved emails,
  saved calls, recordings and every chat (the main chat's older parts too) for a whole-word match. `forget` deletes only
  the confirmed keys (refusing any that aren't in a fresh find), drops chat summaries that name it, hides the chat
  messages (a tool result goes with the reply that called it) and takes them out of stored main-chat parts. Recordings
  go through meetings.remove, so their audio goes too. "Delete everything" now erases recordings' audio as well.
- By voice or chat (`forget_about`): the list comes with a server-written `say` and a confirmId (10 minutes, in memory).
  Deleting needs that id plus the person's own words: in the chat a NEW message from them, on a call what the ear heard
  after the read-back; a call hand-off without an ear never deletes. `forgetAnswer`: a forget word counts as yes only
  without a "don't" before it; any no, keep, nothing/anything, hedge (not sure, maybe, hmm, I don't know) or another
  topic ("about Sam") refuses; everything at once needs "all". The hedge fix also went into approve_by_voice
  (voice-approval.ts `HEDGE`/`saysHedge`, NO now includes not and n't), where "I'm not sure" could approve an email.
- The card ("Privacy and your data", You › Account): four promises, each true with its limits (redaction by shape, a
  call's voice hears speech; always-allow rules; never sold or used for ads; what Neddy keeps). The AI-training sentence
  was removed: OpenRouter isn't pinned to no-training providers, and Soniox/Inworld terms aren't checked. Forget asks
  once more in the same spot ("Keep them" first, "Yes, forget" ignores presses for 600 ms), announces the question and
  puts focus on Keep. Delete everything… is an outline; only the final step is filled red (`Button danger strong`).
### 2. Travel mode (apps/server/src/travel.ts, packages/domain/src/travel.ts, home.tsx TravelCard)
- Abroad = the phone's country (reverse geocode, cached per ~10 km for 6 h) differs from the home area's. Facts for 44
  countries (money, emergency numbers, language), today's rate from open.er-api.com (12 h cache, none if it fails),
  US/UK citizens' lines and embassy finders (CA, AU links). The last country abroad holds 12 h in memory if the signal
  drops; only home turns it off. Nothing about where they've been is stored.
- Chat context line "Travelling right now" (volatile), and calls get the same line with "give only the emergency
  numbers in this line" (liveInstructions `travel`). The card remembers its last answer (without clock times).
### 9. Calm by design (apps/server/src/recap.ts)
