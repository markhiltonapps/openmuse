RECORDER SAFEGUARDS (2026-10-23, owner: "build both safeguards")
-----------------------------------------------------------------
Why: on Oct 23 a 15-minute meeting was lost. The bucket rejected the api's keys, every audio piece got a 502
for 15 minutes, the phone kept the pieces only in memory and the app showed nothing; at Stop the server had no
audio ("No sound reached the server"). Last night's meeting (115766b3) most likely hit the same fault.
1) Keep the audio on the device until the server has it
   - apps/mobile/src/meeting-store.ts: IndexedDB (memory fallback). Each 30 s piece is saved before sending and
     removed once the server has it.
   - apps/mobile/src/meeting-uploader.ts: sends in order, retries with waits (and at once on "online" / app back),
     resumes after a reload, closes the recording on the server only after every piece arrived (job kept on
     the device), keeps pieces the server refuses as "closed" (stuck) so they can be saved as a file, clears
     leftovers after 14 days, lets go of a meeting the server doesn't know.
   - meeting-recorder.ts now hands pieces to the uploader (no queue of its own); Stop waits up to 60 s, then
     keeps the rest on the device (or sooner: "Keep it on this device and carry on").
2) Warn within seconds
   - After 3 failed sends in a row (about a minute in: the first 30 s piece is sent at 30 s): "Not reaching the server" card in the recorder, "· not sending" on the red
     bar, a status line for screen readers, and in the Saving sheet the card + the keep-and-go-on button.
   - The meeting's page shows "Still sending from this device" (about N minutes left, Save a copy of the
     audio) or, when the server had already closed it, "Part of this recording couldn't be sent".
3) Server (apps/server/src/meetings.ts)
   - start() saves and removes a tiny test file first: with storage refusing files no recording starts (503,
     plain words). chunk() answers a refused piece with a 503 in the same plain words (was a bare 502).
Help: new topic "A recording says it isn't sending" (shot recordertrouble, light + dark, marks 1-3) and a line in
"Record a meeting". help-shots.mjs has a recordertrouble shot (fake recorder, server that refuses pieces).
Tests: tests/meetings.test.ts (+1: start refused, piece 503, works again), apps/mobile/test/meeting-uploader.test.ts

Speaker list under Apps › Agent › Voice, shown only when the browser can route sound to a chosen device (`speakerChoiceAvailable`:
HTMLMediaElement.setSinkId plus enumerateDevices; Chrome on desktop and some Android builds, not Safari). The choice (`speaker` in voice
settings, saved on the device) is applied to the live call's audio player (`route` in live-voice.ts) when a call starts and again at once
when changed mid-call; a device that has gone falls back to the default. It does NOT apply to Listen/read-aloud (speechSynthesis can't
be routed). Where the browser can't choose, the card says so and points to Android's media output switcher.
Not verified on a real phone: whether Chrome on Android lists audio outputs and honours setSinkId (the owner's phone will tell us).
Help: topic `microphone` is now "Choose a microphone and speaker" with the new picture `audiodevices` (mocked device lists).
No website change (a settings detail, not a feature people would notice on the home page).
Also: the Inworld TTS ran out of credit on 2026-10-26, and every call ended with "Something went wrong on AI Todd's side" (reason
"unavailable"). Added to to-do, not built: say "the voice provider is out of credit" instead.
Lesson: this container started without node_modules and with a broken system Pillow; `pnpm install --frozen-lockfile` and a venv with
pillow (PATH first) fix both. A stale checkout can be behind the pushed branch: `git fetch` and fast-forward before working.
