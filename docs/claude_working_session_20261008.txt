Owner's feature requests (8), and decisions (asked Oct 8)
1. Location awareness: while the app is open (on a call or not), after one permission. Only the
   latest spot is kept, forgotten after an hour, with a switch to turn it off. Separate from the
   saved home area (weather, local news), which stays as is. No live traffic for now (it needs a
   paid map service such as Google Maps); nearby places use free sources.
2 + 4. Voice approval, one item or several ("approve all", or yes/no for each): everything except
   money (purchases and payments still need a tap). The check listens to the person's own words
   in the call's input transcript, never to the model's decision, so text read aloud (an email
   saying "approve") can't approve. Neddy reads back who and what first.
3. Interrupting mid-sentence: should already work (live voice hears the person while it talks;
   the call's instructions say stop and listen; the call's turns stay in context). Can't call
   from the sandbox: the owner tests it on a call.
5. Urgent alerts mid-call: time to leave, security alerts, money problems, people they choose;
   each with its own switch (and changeable by voice). Open question: whether OpenAI Live lets
   the server make the voice speak unprompted (only testable on Railway); fallback is a tone and
   the alert at the next pause.
6. Rules by voice: already reaches create_routine / create_email_rule through the hand-over; add
   a plain-words read-back before saving.
7. Brief on demand: one "brief me" action reusing the morning brief's sources, short for
   listening.
8. Adaptive reminders: for reminders tied to a calendar event ("30 minutes before the
   dentist"); when the event moves the reminder moves, and the person is told.
Order: 2+4, 6, 7, 5, 8, 1. UI goes through the Design division.

Built: voice approval (2 + 4), committed locally
- voice-approval.ts: read_back_approvals and approve_by_voice, offered only in a call's hand-over
  (thread voice-<session>). The read-back is written by the server from each action (who it goes
  to, the subject, the event and time), up to six at once; anything with money (purchases,
  payment steps, steps needing a typed code) is named as needing a tap. approve_by_voice works
  only on what was just read back (within 5 minutes, once), and only when the person's own words
  since then (LiveVoice.ear: OpenAI's transcript of their microphone, user turns after the
  read-back) say yes. Approving everything needs "all"/"both"; choosing some ("the email, not the
  calendar") can mix yes and no; declining needs a no. It then decides through the usual
  ActionService.decide (same checks as a tap).
- Voice rules: the chat agent on a call uses read_back_approvals, says its text word for word,
  and calls approve_by_voice on their answer; the live voice hands both turns off and never says
  something was approved until the answer says so.
- Help: approvals topic says how to approve by voice.
- Tests: voice-approval.test.ts (5); voice and approval tests (26) still pass.
- Untested on a real call (the sandbox can't reach OpenAI Live): the owner should try "approve it"
  on a call with something waiting.

- Help: routines topic (on a call it's said back first; email rules work the same way).

Built: "brief me" on demand (7), committed locally

  chat, on calls (same agent) and in background jobs (so the scheduled morning brief uses the
  same sources).
- Labels for brief_me, read_back_approvals and approve_by_voice (call and chat "now doing").
- Help: live-call topic (brief me, approving by voice, interrupting).
- Tests: brief.test.ts.

Built: urgent alerts on calls (5) — server committed; the switches card waits for design review

Built: adaptive reminders (8), committed locally
- reminders.ts: set_reminder takes an optional `event` (title, start, id from look_at_calendar);
  the reminder keeps its distance from the event's start. followEvents() runs every 15 minutes
  from maintenance (calendar reads go to the connected apps): if the event moved, the reminder
  moves (never into the past) and the person is told ("“Dentist” moved to …, so your reminder …
  is now at …"); if it can't be found (by id, else by title, within a week either side), they're
  told once and the reminder stays. A calendar that can't be read changes nothing.
- Help: reminders topic.
- Tests: adaptive-reminders.test.ts.

Built: location while the app is open (1) — server committed; the card waits for design review
- whereabouts.ts: on/off setting (off to start); the app reports the phone's spot (POST
  /api/location) only when on; only the latest spot, in memory, never saved; forgotten after an
  hour or when turned off. A street-level name from OpenStreetMap (zoom 17, one a second, cached

- Tools (chat and calls): where_am_i (says when it's off or unknown) and set_location_sharing.
  The live voice hands off "where am I / what's near me". Home area stays for weather and news.
- App (uncommitted until reviewed): LocationReporter (watchPosition only while visible and on;

  voice change applies) and "Your location" card on Apps › Account (asks the browser during the
  tap; says how to unblock it).
- Help: new "location" topic.
- Tests: whereabouts.test.ts (2).

Design review (Oct 8): AI models card round 2 + Urgent alerts card + Your location card
- Visual (UI Designer), usability (UX Researcher) and copy reviewers: three rounds; all three
  signed off. Main fixes from the reviews:
  - Security alerts speak only the sender and subject; plain sign-in code emails aren't urgent;
    runs of 4+ digits are never spoken (dollar amounts are kept).
  - "People you choose" match a whole name or an exact address; removal is exact; 3+ letters.
  - Read-backs say "Number 1: … Number 2: …"; single: "Waiting for your OK: …"; tap-only items
    "can't be approved by voice, so tap Approve on your screen".
  - Routine/email-rule read-backs quote the person's words ("I'll run this request: “…”. Want me
    to set that up?"); time to leave: "starts in about 15 minutes, at 3:00 PM. It's at …".
  - Failed OpenRouter calls say what to do ("OpenRouter is out of credit. Add credit at
    openrouter.ai"); "Saved. Chat now uses GPT-6.1 Sol, thinking hard."
  - Location card: short intro, says the spot is sent to OpenStreetMap, says when the browser
    blocks location, rechecks the setting every 30 s (so a change by voice applies mid-call).
  - Urgent card: Add beside its field, quiet Remove, Enter adds, focus moves to the field after
    Remove, hint about matching names.
