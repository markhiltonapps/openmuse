Details of each step are in docs/claude_working_session_20261107.txt (the device notes run on from there).

What changed today (all pushed; api deployed, firmware zip delivered and installed by the owner):
- Screen: words at the top with a small Neddy tucked at the bottom middle; every drawn window even-aligned (the AMOLED chip shears

--- 2026-11-09 (after midnight UTC, "tomorrow"): touch on the watch ---
Owner's choices: tap Neddy = giggle + eyes follow the finger; press-and-hold Neddy = talk while held; double-tap Neddy = hands-free talk until
the next touch; tap a card = dismiss; swipe left/right = browse the last 5 cards (right = earlier; with no card up, a swipe brings back
the latest). Then the idle face.
- FT3168 touch chip (0x38 on the shared I2C, reset GPIO 9) is read about every 16 ms by polling registers 0x02..0x06 (finger count and X/Y);
  no library. Facts checked in Waveshare's pin table and the open driver source; NOT yet tested on the real board.
- Gestures live in neddy_ui.h (uiTouch), tested on the PC with scripted fingers: firmware/tools/touch_test.cpp (28 checks pass), plus
  giggle/look frames in ui_sim.cpp. A hold or hands-free talk sets uiTouchTalk, which the sketch ORs with the BOOT button.
- FIRST BUILD DRAWS A DOT UNDER THE FINGER (TOUCH_TRAIL 1) so the owner can see whether touch lines up with the screen; if not, one of
  TOUCH_SWAP_XY / TOUCH_FLIP_X / TOUCH_FLIP_Y turns it around. Turn the dots off (TOUCH_TRAIL 0) once confirmed.
- Hold-to-talk starts after about half a second (BOOT is instant); the hold only counts on Neddy.

--- idle face (built, not yet on the watch) ---
Owner's choices: all four parts (big clock + date, today's weather, next meeting, small moving Neddy), after 20 seconds of quiet with
user options, dimming after a minute (wakes on touch or press).
SERVER (needs a push; restarts the api): device-idle.ts builds the idle facts (UTC seconds, the person's offset in minutes, weather in
brief, next timed meeting that hasn't ended, settings) as flat keys; the bridge sends them when a device connects, every 5 minutes and
at once when its settings change. Device settings (devices.ts): idleAfter (0 = never), dim, showWeather, showMeeting, changed by
voice or chat through manage_devices `do: "settings"` (and shown in `list`). The app has NO settings screen for this yet (voice/chat
only): a small section in the device's row would be the next step (with Help).
FIRMWARE: neddy_ui.h idle face (clock in the TEMP font, date, weather row with a small picture, meeting title and "in 25 min  4:40 PM",
small Neddy below; the clock drifts a few pixels every few minutes against burn-in; redraws once a minute); dims with the display
chip's brightness command 0x51 through the bus (uiOut.brightness); any touch, press, words or card wakes it and it starts fresh.
Checked on the PC: idle renders (5 variants), timing, dimming and waking, even windows; the sketch's reader run against the server's
real idle message. NOT verified on the board: the brightness command (CO5300 0x51), and the clock needs the server's message first
(without it the watch shows no idle face).
Tests: device-idle (new, 5), devices, device-card, weather, day-tools, voice-brain, conversation-device: 44 pass; tsc, biome clean.

Watch idle-screen by voice (owner test): asked "make the watch idle screen come on after 30 seconds" and the agent said it couldn't.
Cause (owner found it): the agent only knew "device", not "watch". Fixed: manage_devices description and DEVICE_SCREEN say "watch",
`pick` ignores kind words (watch, wearable, screen...) unless a device is really named that, the voice service's DEVICE_RULES says
watch requests are handed off. New test in tests/device-idle.test.ts. Held locally until "push it" (api + voice both redeploy).

Idle face not showing / not dimming on the real watch (owner): the first cause was an older build still on the board (the touch dot was
still on). Flashed the newer zip: dot gone, but no dim after ~2 minutes (dim comes 60 s after the idle face appears, so ~90 s of quiet
with a 30 s setting). Added NEDDY_IDLE_DEBUG (default 1 in this test build): one line at the bottom, "g1 a30 m0 k0 i0 d10 q12" =
got idle facts, idle-after seconds, mode (0 ready), card on, idle face on, dim on/dimmed, quiet seconds. Turn it to 0 once it works.
Zip rebuilt, committed locally, not pushed (a push restarts the api).

FOUND: owner's photo showed "g1 a30 m0 k0 i0 d10 q6": settings arrived, ready, no card, but quiet only 6 s after minutes. Cause: uiSetDiag
(Wi-Fi RSSI etc., changing every second) went through uiCopy, which counts any text change as activity, so the quiet timer restarted forever.
Fix: uiCopyQuiet for diagnostics. New check in firmware/tools/touch_test.cpp (31 checks): idle face comes while diag lines change, dims 60 s
later, a touch wakes it and restores brightness 255; it fails (3) on the old code. The idle debug line stays on one more test round.

Idle face and dimming confirmed working on the real watch (after pushing 6f46230 and flashing the fixed zip).
Owner asked about a text flicker every second: the diagnostic lines (Wi-Fi strength, timings; and the idle test line) changed every second and each change
marked the whole text area dirty, so the words were blanked and redrawn once a second. Fix: diagnostics only mark a small band dirty (uiDiagDirty), redrawn
at most every ~1 s and only when shown; nothing when hidden. New checks in firmware/tools/touch_test.cpp (33 checks): fail (2) on the old code. The idle
test line is now off (NEDDY_IDLE_DEBUG 0), so the bottom lines are gone unless NEDDY_DIAG is set to 1. Zip rebuilt; committed locally, not pushed.

Watch home screen (owner's answers: most user friendly; 10 seconds; bright while recording; small battery indicator):

  (voice: show_battery; idle fact `sb`), tool/description call it the "home screen". Tests in tests/device-idle.test.ts (18 pass with devices tests).
- Firmware: cards now last as long as the idle wait (min 10 s; a minute when the face is off) so the face returns right after an answer or card;
  battery indicator under the meeting line: outline + fill + percent, teal "charging", amber at 15% or less, hidden with no battery (uiDrawBattery,
  uiSetBattery); the sketch reads the AXP2101 (I2C 0x34: 0x00 bit3 battery present, 0x01 bits 6:5 = 01 charging, 0xA4 percent) every 30 s.
  Register meanings are from XPowersLib's constants; NOT verified on the real board: turn NEDDY_IDLE_DEBUG to 1 to see "b<percent> <status1> <status2>".
  Brightness already returns to full on any touch, press or words, so it is bright while listening and talking; nothing is tied to charging.
