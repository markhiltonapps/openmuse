## Receipt prompts after eating out (owner: "know when I'm at a restaurant … and ask me if I want to upload a receipt")
Owner's choices (asked): build all three (card charge prompt, restaurant visit prompt, emailed receipts filed on their
own), for every restaurant. Built in apps/server/src/receipt-nudges.ts; committed locally, not pushed.

- Card charges: every 30 minutes per person with Brex connected (Composio BREX_GET_CARD_TRANSACTIONS, limit 50), purchases
  with business codes 5811-5814 (restaurants, bars, fast food and coffee), up to 3 days old, amounts from cents. The
  merchant name is cleaned from the card descriptor ("SQ *MARLOWS TAVERN SAN FRANCISCO CA" → "Marlows Tavern").
  UNTESTED against a real Brex answer: the parser walks any wrapping (data.items, response_data.items…). Check Railway's
  log for "Couldn't read Brex card charges" after the first push.
- Visits: Whereabouts.onReport → 20+ minutes within ~80 m (plus accuracy), a restaurant or café within 50 m on
  OpenStreetMap (Nearby, two Overpass looks at most per stay, skipped when the fix is rougher than 60 m), then a report
  from elsewhere within 3 hours. Only while the app is open; nothing about the visit is kept beyond the question itself.
- One meal = one question: the visit and the card charge on the same day with a matching name are one; nothing is asked
  when a matching receipt is already in the box (day ±1, name word or amount within a third, tips allowed).
- Every question goes through the proactive judge, new area "receipts" ("Receipts after eating out"; it shows under
  When Neddy speaks first › Kinds of notice automatically). Held ones are retried every 10 minutes. Marked asked
  before the notice goes out.
- Notice: "Add the receipt for {merchant}?" with extra receiptNudgeId → push URL /?receipt=<id>; the bell button reads
  "Snap the receipt". The Receipt box opens with a question card: Snap it / No receipt / Ask me later / Stop asking
  about receipts. Snap it closes the question only when a photo arrives (a cancelled camera keeps it). Later asks once
  more after 3 hours (new notice key); a second Later leaves it in the updates. Stop pauses the area for 30 days.
  Routes: GET/POST /api/receipt-box/nudges/:id.
- Voice: receipt_box tool `do: "question"` lists open questions or answers none/later (merchant picks one), in the chat
  and on calls.
- Emailed receipts: plain code picks senders by real address (uber, doordash, grubhub, seamless, postmates, caviar,
  toasttab, square, opentable, resy, lyft, instacart, chownow, gopuff, deliveroo, just-eat, grab, clover, tock,
  sevenrooms) plus a receipt subject. They come from emails the chat reads (emailViews.onRead) and a Gmail sweep every
  6 hours (newer_than:2d, 15 results). ReceiptBox.fromEmail reads the text with the simple-jobs model (hideText first),
  keeps the email in Files as .txt and files on its own when everything is clear (otherwise To check). Each email once;
  the hourly read cap is checked before it's marked, so a capped one is read on a later sweep. Outlook isn't swept yet.
  These show "From an email" (Mail tile, subject, sender, Open the email) instead of a photo; photoUrl is null.

# Watch redesign: the competitor's round-watch ideas, on the round 1.75C and the 2.06 (owner, 2026-11-27)

The owner sent photos of a competitor's round watch UI and asked to take its ideas into Neddy's watch. Answers to the
questions: all four parts (round-screen look, settings one per page, launcher around Neddy, recording modes + info),
"let people choose" the colour (amber default), all at once, support BOTH boards starting with the round one (the owner
has a Waveshare ESP32-S3-Touch-AMOLED-1.75C), and the recording modes filed as proposed (note / idea / meeting / dream).
Taken: the patterns. Left out: their robot art, brand and exact look; a language switch (English only); Do not disturb
(the watch gets no notifications yet); "Check for updates" (the watch has no over-the-air update: no OTA partitions).

## Firmware (firmware/neddy_device)
- neddy_board.h (new): NEDDY_BOARD 206 or 175 (settings.h); pins, display column offset, touch chip, PWR pin per board.
  1.75C facts from Waveshare's own repo/schematic/BSP (researched 2026-11-27): same CO5300, AXP2101, QMI8658, ES8311,
  ES7210; touch CST9217 at 0x5A (read D0 00 -> 15 bytes, ack D0 00 AB, mirrored x and y), column offset 6, touch reset
  GPIO2, PWR read on GPIO3 (GPIO10 is the mic line there), I2S 9/45/8/10/16. Unchecked on a real 1.75C: PWR level on
  GPIO3, the IMU's axes (raise to wake), and the CST9217 read (written from SensorLib's driver).
- neddy_draw.h (new): soft-edged shapes painted in strips, text along a circle (curved titles), tracked capitals,
  line icons, page dots (arc on round, row/column on the rectangle), the screen's shape (uiHalfAt, uiEdgePoint).
- neddy_shell.h (new): the launcher (PWR), Record pages, Settings pages (9), Info pages (3), the double press of PWR.
- neddy_ui.h: screen size from NEDDY_ROUND; accent colour (amber/blue/green/pink) replaces teal; brightness setting;
  screen off after N seconds; round layouts (Neddy's sizes, words, idle faces, cards on a centred 410-wide canvas without
  panels, setup QR screens); new recording screens. Old tile menu and volume screen removed.
- neddy_rec.h: the recording's mode in the header ("MD" + mode; older headers read as meeting). Upload adds &mode=.
- neddy_device.ino: board pins, CST9217 read, PWR on the board's pin through uiPwrPress (double press), all settings sent
  to and read from the server (a 4 s guard so an older echo doesn't undo a change made on the watch), Restart, Info's
  live values. Version 0.2.0.
- Tests: touch_test 130 checks pass on BOTH shapes (launcher, settings drag/steppers/choices, record modes, double press,
  info/restart, face, weather, screen off, hold to talk, timeouts); rec_test 19 (mode kept); raise_test, qr_test pass;
  ui_sim draws every screen for both shapes with 0 odd windows. The sketch passes g++ -fsyntax-only for both boards with
  stand-in Arduino headers (scratchpad/stub): names and types only, not the real core (not downloadable here). The check
  found one real bug (the sleep code still used the old pending-setting names), fixed.
